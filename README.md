\# FloodReason-BD



\## Robust Multimodal Flood Impact Assessment for Bangladesh



FloodReason-BD is a research project focused on robust flood impact assessment in Bangladesh using multimodal evidence.



The project investigates how satellite imagery, hydro-meteorological observations, and Bangla crisis/public reports can be combined to estimate flood impact and remain reliable when one or more information sources are missing or noisy.



\## Core Modalities



\- Sentinel-1 SAR imagery

\- Optical satellite imagery where available

\- Rainfall and other meteorological observations

\- River water-level observations

\- Bangla public/social/news evidence



\## Research Focus



1\. Multimodal flood impact assessment

2\. Missing-modality robustness

3\. Event-aligned multimodal data

4\. Bangladesh-specific flood analysis

5\. Explainable and evidence-grounded predictions
